The problem

When job ads are not working, another ad will not help.

Most clients contact us after they have tried the usual channels and seen the active candidates. At that point, we do not post another ad. The people you need usually have a job, do not apply and do not appear through the usual channels. But they are willing to talk about the right offer.

The hardest person to find is rarely the director. Directors tend to be visible. The hardest is the specialist who has a job, is not looking and would probably ignore a standard offer. That is why they never answer a job ad.

A different kind of work

A typical agency works with whoever applies. We work with the people who never would.

The active part of the market is made up of people who are looking right now, answering ads or open to offers. We also work with people who have a job, do not apply and never appear through the usual channels. They have a strong profile not because they want a new job, but because they are good at the one they have. We approach them directly.

Defining the role

Describe the role the wrong way and the right people slip through the net.

The market may use a different name for the role than the one you have in mind. One badly worded requirement is enough to lose a candidate who can do four of the five things you need. Not because they are unsuitable, but because the brief was aimed in the wrong place.

So we start by clarifying who you really need: what the person must be able to do, what experience they must have, what environment they will work in and how you will know they are succeeding.

Accuracy over volume

It is not about the number of CVs. It is about how many are worth an interview.

We do not send dozens of profiles and leave you to guess why they arrived. We present each candidate with context: why they match the brief, what is worth checking and why the conversation is worth having.
