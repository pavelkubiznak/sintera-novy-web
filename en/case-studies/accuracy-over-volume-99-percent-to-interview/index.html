Case study

Accuracy over volume: 99% of candidates go to interview

Quality managers and quality engineers · Manufacturer of automotive safety systems · Czech Republic
Situation
For quality roles, there is no point in wasting time sorting through irrelevant CVs.
Why standard recruitment was not enough
In these roles, precise targeting is what counts. A high volume of CVs does not help if the candidates do not meet the requirements.
What we did
Sintera presents candidates who meet the requirements of the role.
Result
99% of the candidates presented go on to interview, and most positions are filled through Sintera.

This story is based on a client reference. We do not name the company. The field, the type of roles and the region are real.
