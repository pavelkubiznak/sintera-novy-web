Roles we fill

From CNC setters and programmers to production directors

Sintera does not only look for managers. A direct approach (direct search) makes sense for every role that a job ad will not fill, and in manufacturing and engineering most roles are like this. Below are positions we have filled or are filling now, by level and by field. The open positions themselves are published in Czech.

Skilled trades and production roles

We look for machine setters, CNC programmers and milling operators, welders, fitters, toolmakers and electromechanical technicians with the same care as for managers. These are exactly the people who have a job, whom companies want to keep and who do not respond to job ads.

Frequently asked questions

Do you only fill management positions?

No. A large part of our work is skilled trade, production and technical roles: CNC programmers and CNC machine operators, machine setters, milling operators, welders, fitters, toolmakers, electromechanical technicians and maintenance technicians. We also fill roles for quality engineers, design engineers, manufacturing engineers, PLC programmers and other specialists. We fill foreman, production manager and production director roles too.

Do you also find CNC programmers, machine setters and welders?

Yes. We regularly fill roles for CNC programmers, CNC milling operators, horizontal boring machine operators, setters of CNC lathes and vertical lathes, welders, machine fitters and toolmakers, across the whole Czech Republic. Current positions are published in Czech at https://www.sintera.cz/pozice/.
