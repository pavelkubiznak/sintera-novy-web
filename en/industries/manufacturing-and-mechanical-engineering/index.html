Direct search in manufacturing and mechanical engineering

We look for machine setters, CNC programmers, welders, manufacturing engineers, foremen and production managers. We approach them directly, because good people in production have a job and do not read job ads.

Why these roles are hard to fill

An experienced machine setter or CNC programmer is hard for a company to replace. They are not actively looking for a job. When they do consider a change, they decide based on the specific machines, the shift pattern, the commute and the people they will work with. A general ad such as “CNC operator wanted” will not reach them.

It is similar with leadership roles in production. A foreman or production manager who can lead people and also understands the technology is rarely available on the market. These people need to be found and approached, and they need to hear why a change makes sense for them.

How we work

We translate your brief into the language of the market: which machines and control systems, what type of production (single-piece or series production), what tolerances and what shift pattern. Based on this, we search in companies where the same work is actually done, and we give candidates concrete reasons to talk to you.

  1. We translate your brief into the language of the market: the real content of the job, the team, the conditions and what makes the role interesting.
  2. We map the companies and positions where suitable people work today.
  3. We approach them directly, including people who are not actively looking for a job.
  4. We present a shortlist with a comment on each candidate: why they match, their motivation, availability and expectations.

We usually present the first candidates matched to your brief within 10 days after we agree on the role profile together. A named consultant is responsible for each search.

Frequently asked questions

Do you fill technical and skilled trade roles, or only management?

Both. Most of the production roles we fill are specialists and technicians: machine setters, CNC programmers, welders, manufacturing engineers and fitters. We also look for foremen, production managers and production directors.

How quickly will you present the first candidates?

Usually within 10 days after we agree on the role profile together. The candidates are matched to your brief.

Do you have a role you cannot fill?

Send us the job description or a few sentences about the role. We will get back to you and suggest how to approach it.
