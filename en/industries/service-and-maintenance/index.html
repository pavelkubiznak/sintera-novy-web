Industries · Service and maintenance

Direct search for service and maintenance

We look for maintenance technicians, electrical maintenance specialists, production line maintenance specialists, field service technicians and service managers.

Why these roles are hard to fill

A maintenance technician who understands electrics, mechanics and line control is key for a plant, and the company will not let them go. They rarely look for a change.

A field service technician also needs technical knowledge, a driving licence, often a foreign language and a willingness to travel. It is hard to find this combination through a job ad.

How we work

We clarify the type of equipment, the shift pattern, the share of electrical and mechanical work, and the amount of travel. We approach people from plants and service organisations that look after the same equipment.

  1. We translate your brief into the language of the market: the real content of the job, the team, the conditions and what makes the role interesting.
  2. We map the companies and positions where suitable people work today.
  3. We approach them directly, including people who are not actively looking for a job.
  4. We present a shortlist with a comment on each candidate: why they match, their motivation, availability and expectations.

We usually present the first candidates matched to your brief within 10 days of the moment we agree on the role profile together. A named consultant is responsible for each search.
